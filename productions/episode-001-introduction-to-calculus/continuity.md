## Notation Choices

| Symbol/Format | Usage Context | Rationale | Consistency Check |
|---------------|---------------|-----------|-------------------|
| dp/dt (Leibniz) | Primary notation throughout | Reminds viewer of "change in position over change in time"; standard in physics/engineering | ✓ Used exclusively; no mixed notation with d/dx or f'(t) |
| p(t) for position | Function definition | Clear, unambiguous; avoids confusion with y = mx + b convention | ✓ Consistent from Shot 10 onward |
| h as interval | Difference quotient | Standard calculus convention; distinguishes from Δt which is finite change | ✓ Only used in limit context (Shots 11-12) |
