## Description

In this episode, we explore the fundamental concept of derivatives in calculus. By the end, you'll understand exactly what a derivative means in physical terms—not just as abstract symbols on a page.

**What you'll learn:**
- The difference between average velocity and instantaneous velocity
- How derivatives measure instantaneous rate of change
- Leibniz notation (dp/dt) and its intuitive meaning
- The formal limit definition behind the concept

This episode is designed for high school AP Calculus students, self-learners interested in mathematical thinking, or anyone who's ever wondered what their speedometer really means.

**Next episode:** Episode 2 will show you how to compute actual derivatives using rules rather than limits from scratch.
