# Cracked — Every riddle hides a law. Break it open.

**Series slug:** `cracked`
**Target audience:** Class 6–12 students (ages 11–17), Indian CBSE/NCERT curriculum
**Total episodes:** 455

## Name Rationale

"Cracked" captures the dual meaning of cracking a puzzle open and the crack/snap of sudden insight when understanding arrives. It is one word, YouTube-friendly, works for ages 11–17, and signals a resolution — something satisfying happens at the end. Unlike "Brain Teasers" or "Math Puzzles" it promises a reveal, not just a challenge. Colloquially the word also means brilliant or slightly wild, which suits a series that presents seemingly absurd claims before proving them rigorously.

## Series Description

A riddle-based cross-curriculum series for Class 6–12. Each episode presents a paradoxical or counter-intuitive scenario as a riddle, withholds the concept name until the visual resolution reveal, then resolves it through Manim animation. The concept is never named in the opening — only introduced once the riddle has created the need for it. Episodes span mathematics, physics, and their intersection, drawing on exponential growth, topology, standing waves, probability, and more.

## Format Notes

- **Hook** — the riddle question. Never state the concept name. The opening poses a scenario that feels wrong or impossible.
- **Resolution** — the concept reveal animation. Manim renders the proof visually; narration describes what the viewer can see, never substitutes for it.
- **Worked example rule** — one numerical example must be fully worked on-screen, not merely stated.
- **Cross-curriculum tagging** — each episode carries a `classLevel` tag so it can be surfaced in the correct school-year playlists or watched standalone.
